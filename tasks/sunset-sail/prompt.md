Build `index.html`: an animated scene of a sailing ship on the sea at sunset.

## Requirements

- Everything is inside `index.html`. No other files and no external scripts, styles, fonts or images: the page is opened offline.
- The scene is drawn on one `<canvas>` that fills the browser window, with no scrollbars, and follows the window when it is resized.
- It shows a sky with a low sun, a sea with visible waves, and a sailing ship with a hull, at least one mast and sails.
- The water moves continuously, driven by `requestAnimationFrame`. Animate anything else you like.
- The browser console shows no errors.

## How it is judged

The requirements above are checked automatically. Beyond that, people compare screenshots of different attempts side by side and pick the better-looking one, so make the lighting, reflections, colour and composition as good as you can.
